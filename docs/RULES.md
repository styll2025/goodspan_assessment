# How a plan is built (plain English)

Codes (P2, P5…) match `data/rules.json` and the Assessment mapping sheet in `source/practice_library_and_mapping.xlsx`.

1. **Safety and context first.** Answers set *conditions* (e.g. pregnancy, kidney disease, shift work, exercise warning symptoms). Any practice whose `exclude_if` contains a condition is removed. Equipment practices need a gym, studio or club. "Practices you'd rather not do" remove matching practices. **P11 holds**: Expressive writing (Moderate, Deep), the alcohol-free week and Alcohol intake are not offered until the Pilot releases them (the assessment no longer screens mood or alcohol). Issues the rules can't resolve become **Pilot flags**; nothing is shown to the member.
2. **Starting level (P2, P3).** Baseline answers map to Gentle / Moderate / Deep (or *skip* if already at the top). A sleep habit the member hasn't tried counts as Gentle. Families with no baseline start at the member's typical level in that pillar (median, max Moderate). Something the member already does starts at Moderate at least and never moves down. "Small steps" keeps new practices at Gentle; "bigger challenge" starts one level up.
3. **Personalisation score (P5).** Top goal +100, other goals +40, named sleep disruptor +40, room to improve +4 to +20, sleep problem +15, barrier +10, enjoyed activity or place +8, working from home +10 (sitting, time off work, connection), Balance for 60+ +20, tried and didn't help −30. Ties: evidence fit, evidence strength, fewer minutes, then ID. Never random, never library order.
4. **Top goal (P4).** The selected goal whose practices show the most room to improve; ties → questionnaire order.
5. **Focus pillars.** The member's 1–2 choices; "I'm not sure yet" → the two pillars with the highest *average* room to improve across their own baseline questions (+10 per goal chosen).
6. **Months 1–3 (P12).** Six practices a month. Two focus pillars: 2 each + 2 light-touch rotating through the other three pillars. One focus pillar: 2 + 4 light-touch (one per other pillar). The first practice in each focus pillar matches its top goal; the second comes from a different theme if one scores 40+. Light-touch practices start Gentle. **Sharing sleep progress (P9)** is light-touch only and only in a month with a Sleep practice.
7. **Weekly time (P7).** Budget from the time question (60 / 120 / 240 / 300 min) × 1.25. Practices of ≤10 min a week are micro and always allowed. Over budget: hold step-ups, then lower or swap lower-priority practices for micro ones; keep the top-goal practice; still over → Pilot flag. Under 45 min (not small steps): step a focus practice up.
8. **Monthly progression (P6).** Done → next level (after Deep: the linked next family if there is one, e.g. Building up cardio 30 → 60 → 100 min then Weekly cardio 150 → 200 → 300 min; otherwise the next best family); partly → repeat; not yet → one level easier (or swap at Gentle); not for me → swap, never offered again in months 1–3.
9. **Months 4–6 themes (P14).** Four themes: one per focus pillar (two if one focus pillar) + two from other pillars, each where the member's practices went best (else best-scoring). Sharing Sleep Progress is never a theme. Practices inside themes are chosen at mid-Span.
10. **Foundations checklist (P13).** Up to five hygiene items triggered by specific answers (e.g. noise affects sleep → "cut bedroom noise"). They don't count towards the six practices and are shown as a checklist outside the plan (currently at the end).
12. **References.** The plan lists the evidence for every practice family in months 1–3 and every foundation shown (`references` in the plan output).
11. **Pilot asks every member** about mood, alcohol, falls (60+) and long-term sleep problems at the pre-Span interview, then sets Pilot conditions and releases holds.
