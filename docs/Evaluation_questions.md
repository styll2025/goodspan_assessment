# The Good Span evaluation

How the assessment stands on 5 November 2026. This is the live questionnaire at [evaluation.thegoodspan.com](https://evaluation.thegoodspan.com/). The wording below is the wording people see.

There are **55 questions** in **9 sections**. A question that does not apply is skipped, so one person does not see every question. The numbers below are the order in the assessment. The number on screen moves with the questions that apply to that person.

## How an answer works

- **Choose one.** One answer.
- **Choose all that apply.** Several answers. On a goal question the limit is three, and on the opening question it is two.
- **One answer for each row.** A grid. Each row is its own short question in the conversation, and a table in the form.
- **Something else** and **Other** open a short box: “Tell us a little more, if you’d like.” Up to 300 characters. The words go to the Pilot. They do not add a practice, count as a habit they already do, or add a new type of practice to leave out.
- **None of these**, **Nothing in particular**, and **I’m not sure yet** stand alone. Choosing one clears the other answers on that question.
- Two questions are optional and can be left blank: the personal habit, and the final note.

## What has changed

These are the updates now in the live evaluation.

| Question | What it is now |
|---|---|
| Opening question | “Which one or two areas would you most like to improve?” Choose up to 2. |
| Recent symptoms | Includes Something else, with a few words. |
| Working pattern | Includes Other, with a few words. Other is not treated as shift work, irregular hours, or not working. |
| Salt | “Consuming more than 5 g of salt per day, including salt added during cooking and at the table.” |
| Eating goals | Includes Something else, with a few words. |
| Sleep habits | The current-habit answer is “I already do this”. Every row, including working with a professional or a sleep programme such as CBT-I, includes “Not practical for me right now”. |
| Tobacco | Cigarettes and vapes are separate rows. The combined “I don’t smoke or vape” answer is gone. |
| Sun and hearing | The question “Which of these apply to you?” is gone. Hearing aids are still asked from age 60. Sun protection and hearing remain as prevention goals. |
| Sleep, movement, mind, connection, prevention, enjoyment, and practices to leave out | Something else, with a few words, on the questions listed in the sections below. |
| Learning | “About once a month” sits between “Rarely or never” and “A few times a month”. |
| End of the assessment | An optional note: “Is there anything else you think we should know or consider when creating your personalised plan?” |
