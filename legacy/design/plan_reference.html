SleepLight touch
5 minutes of progressive muscle relaxation before bed: tense and release each muscle group in turn.
Lying in bed, work from your feet up to your face. Tense each muscle group firmly (never to the point of pain) for a few seconds, then let go and notice the release. Moving through your feet, legs, stomach, hands, arms, shoulders and face takes about 5 minutes.
